Franco Conte is a sound designer based in Milan, working across commercial campaigns, fashion films, luxury brand projects and immersive media. He collaborates with production companies, creative agencies and independent directors on both commissioned and research-driven projects.

His sound design practice spans advertising, fashion film and high-end brand content, with a specific focus on sound for luxury and contemporary visual culture. Alongside commercial work, he develops audio for immersive technologies — VR, AR, interactive installations and spatial audio systems.

Practice

Every project begins with a close reading of the image — its rhythm, texture and emotional register. Sound design, in this approach, is not illustrative but structural: it shapes how a scene is experienced, not just heard.

Work has been presented at Triennale di Milano, Piccolo Teatro, LAC Lugano and MEET Digital Culture Center. Alongside client work, ongoing research explores generative audio, binaural sound and real-time music interaction.
