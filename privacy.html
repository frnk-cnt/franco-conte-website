Policy

This privacy policy describes how franco-conte.com ("the site") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit. The site is owned and operated by Franco Conte, based in Milan, Italy. This policy complies with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Italian Privacy Code (D.Lgs. 196/2003 as amended by D.Lgs. 101/2018).

What data is collected

This site does not directly collect personal data. No registration, no forms, no tracking pixels. However, the following third-party services may process data when you use the site:

Cloudflare (hosting): The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process your IP address and basic request data for security and performance purposes. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

YouTube (video embeds): Videos on this site are embedded via YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube does not set tracking cookies unless you interact with a video player. If you play a video, YouTube may collect data according to Google's privacy policy.

Fonts: All fonts used on this site are served locally from our own server. No requests are made to Google Fonts or any other external font service.

Cookies

This site uses a single functional cookie (fc_cookie_choice) to remember your cookie consent preference. This cookie contains no personal data and expires at the end of your browser session.

No analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party tracking cookies are set. YouTube may set cookies if you interact with an embedded video — this is governed by Google's cookie policy.

Legal basis for processing

Any data processing that occurs is based on legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for the purpose of delivering the website securely and correctly, or on your explicit consent where required.

Data retention

The site does not store personal data. Server logs maintained by Cloudflare are subject to their own retention policies.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data. Since this site does not collect personal data directly, most of these rights apply to third-party services listed above. For any privacy-related requests, contact info@franco-conte.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in services used. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
