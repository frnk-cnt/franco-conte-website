I'm Franco Conte, a sound designer, composer, and audiovisual programmer based in Milan. I design and curate sound for commercials, VR experiences, performing arts and podcasting projects.

My background, combined with a passion for the tech world, has allowed me to develop an approach to audio contaminated by new technologies, such as audio generative AI and binaural audio.

I lectured a workshop on interactive dance alongside AiEP at Seoul Institute of Arts, and have presented work at Piccolo Teatro, Triennale di Milano, Lugano's LAC, MEET Digital Culture Center and Teatro Comandini.
